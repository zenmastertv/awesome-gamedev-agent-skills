Patterns in that data:

- Game Boy Color projects raised about 3-10x more than Game Boy-only ones.
- Goals are small (USD 2.5k-6k) and the strong projects reach 10x or more.
- A clear one-line genre hook wins. Compilations and vague pitches struggle.
- Horror, humour, cozy and 90s nostalgia funded well.
- Physical cartridge + box + manual is the tier every campaign sells. Most showed a demo first.

## Ideas, community and market

- itch.io Game Boy Color games: https://itch.io/games/tag-gameboy-color
- itch.io GB Studio games: https://itch.io/games/made-with-gb-studio
- itch.io Game Boy jams: https://itch.io/jams/tag-gameboy
- GB Compo, the gbdev yearly competition: https://gbdev.io/gbcompo/
- gbdev resource list: https://gbdev.io/resources.html
- Homebrew Hub, a large playable archive: https://hh.gbdev.io/
- GB Studio community and showcases: https://www.gbstudio.dev/ and https://gbstudiocentral.com/
- r/Gameboy and r/gbstudio on Reddit, for what players share and ask for.
- Physical publishers to study (pricing, packaging, presentation): Incube8 Games
  (https://www.incube8.games/), Mega Cat Studios (https://megacatstudios.com/),
  First Press Games (https://www.firstpressgames.com/).
- ModRetro Chromatic is new GBC-compatible hardware; a game that plays well on it is a selling
  point. Check its current publishing programme before promising anything.
